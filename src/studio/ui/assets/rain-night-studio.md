# Rain-night Studio illustration

`rain-night-studio.png` is an original illustration generated for this project's
launcher with the built-in `image_gen` tool. It is a local asset; the launcher
does not download images or contact an image service at startup.

The user requested a rainy-night convenience store, then supplied a style
reference and asked for reduced visual noise and a restrained palette. The
final revision uses dark teal, broad painted surfaces and a small amount of
pale warm shop light. The user reference itself is not distributed. Earlier
space-station and more colorful store variants are not shipped.

Final generation prompt (Image 1 was the original generated store; Image 2 was
the user-provided style reference):

> Use case: style-transfer. Image 1 is the edit target: the portrait rainy-night convenience-store illustration. Image 2 is the user's STYLE AND PALETTE REFERENCE only. Restyle Image 1 substantially toward Image 2: quiet hand-drawn anime environment with a very restrained low-saturation teal/blue-green palette, nearly black green silhouettes, and small pale warm-yellow shop light. Keep a rainy-night convenience store as the unmistakable main subject and keep portrait 1024x1536 format for a desktop launcher's art panel. Simplify the overly dense tiny shelf details, individual droplets, glittering ground and high-frequency textures into much broader coherent painted shapes, clean planes and larger subdued reflections. Reduce microdetail and noise strongly. Remove rich orange, royal blue, violet, pink highlights, neon glow and photorealistic surface detail. The rain should be suggested by atmosphere and a few soft strokes, not hundreds of bright lines. The store windows can contain simplified silhouettes of shelves. Use a quiet green-gray sky/backdrop with generous clean dark negative space in the top quarter, a small inviting shop below, subtle puddles and a dark tree or utility pole silhouette to structure depth. Controlled values, soft mist, gentle fluorescent pale-yellow interior illumination. Aim for the understated cinematic melancholy and limited-color visual language of Image 2, with legible architectural drawing rather than photorealism. Do not copy Image 2's exact shop branding, vehicle, city pattern, or any player notification overlay. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no people needed, no UI. The resulting image should feel simpler, quieter and much less colorful than Image 1.
